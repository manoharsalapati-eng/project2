Extrudate conveyor dryer: EKF soft sensor & constrained MPC

A twin-screw extruder feeds wet extrudate onto a hot-air belt dryer. Three identical plant twins run side by side under the same disturbances and the same sensor noise, one per control strategy: open-loop recipe, PID and constrained nonlinear MPC. Each twin has its own Extended Kalman Filter that infers the hidden core moisture, product temperature and browning from noisy exhaust-air temperature and humidity.

Model, estimator and controller equations

1 · Non-linear plant: lumped belt-dryer zone (5 states)

States: core moisture X (kg/kg dry basis), product temperature Tp, exhaust (chamber) air temperature Ta, exhaust absolute humidity Y and Maillard browning index B. Inputs: inlet air temperature Tin and dry-air flow G. Disturbances: raw-feed moisture Xin, ambient humidity Yamb and temperature Tamb, and throughput Fs.

a_w(X) = r/(1+r), r = (X/A)^(1/n) Oswin sorption isotherm (A = 0.12, n = 0.35) Y_s = 0.622·a_w·p_sat(T_p) / (P − a_w·p_sat(T_p)) surface equilibrium humidity hA = hA₀·√(G/G₀), k_yA = hA / c_pH convective transfer, Lewis analogy R_d = k_yA · X/(X+X_D) · (Y_s − Y) drying rate with internal-diffusion (falling-rate) factor M_s·dX/dt = F_s(X_in − X) − R_d M_s(c_ps+c_pw·X)·dT_p/dt = F_s(c_ps+c_pw·X_in)(T_feed − T_p) + hA(T_a − T_p) − R_d·λ(T_p) M_a·c_pH·dT_a/dt = G(c_pa+c_pv·Y_amb)(T_in − T_a) − hA(T_a − T_p) − R_d·c_pv(T_a − T_p) − UA(T_a − T_amb) M_a·dY/dt = G(Y_amb − Y) + R_d dB/dt = (F_s/M_s)(B_in − B) + k_B·exp[−E_a/R·(1/T_p − 1/T_ref)]·exp[−((a_w − 0.65)/0.25)²]

The true plants are integrated with RK4 (1 s steps). Their heat-transfer, diffusion, wall-loss and kinetic parameters differ from the controller's model by the mismatch slider. The air and evaporation modes are stiff (time constants near 1 s), so the MPC predictor uses an L-stable second-order Rosenbrock (ROS2, W-method) integrator.

2 · Extended Kalman Filter soft sensor (6 states)

z = [X, T_p, T_a, Y, B, X_in]ᵀ X_in is an unmeasured random-walk disturbance state y = [T_a, RH(T_a, Y)]ᵀ + v, v ~ N(0, diag(0.4², 1.2²)) Predict: ẑ⁻ = f_d(ẑ, u, d̂), F = ∂f_d/∂z (finite differences), P⁻ = F P Fᵀ + Q Update: K = P⁻Hᵀ(HP⁻Hᵀ + R)⁻¹, ẑ = ẑ⁻ + K(y − h(ẑ⁻)), P = (I−KH)P⁻(I−KH)ᵀ + KRKᵀ (Joseph form)

Browning does not affect either measurement, so it is not observable. It is detectable: its washout dynamics are stable, and the EKF propagates its estimate and covariance from the estimated Tp and X. Q includes a model-error allowance so the ±2σ bands stay close to 95 % consistent at the default 5 % mismatch.

3 · Controllers

Open-loop : T_in, G fixed at the steady-state recipe for the set-point (fixed-airflow slider) PID : velocity form on the EKF moisture estimate, e = X̂ − X_sp (reverse acting), Δu = K_p[Δe + (T_s/T_i)e + (T_d/T_s)Δ²X̂] (derivative on PV, clamped, slew-limited), G fixed MPC : min Σ_k w_X(X_k − X_sp)² + w_E·(Q_heater,k + P_fan,k)/P_ref + w_B·max(0, B_k − B_max + 1)² + w_Δu·Σ‖Δz‖² s.t. T_p,k ≤ T_max − δ − 2σ_Tp (hard, every step of the 15-min horizon) 60 ≤ T_in ≤ 200 °C, 2 ≤ G ≤ 8 kg/s, slew limits horizon 60 × 15 s, 5 move blocks [2,4,8,16,30], RTI-SQP (2 iterations) + ADMM QP, then a bisection safety filter that lowers T_in until the nonlinear prediction is feasible.

4 · KPIs

IAE = ∫|Xw.b. − Xsp| dt on the true outlet moisture. Specific energy = ∫(heater + fan power) dt ÷ product mass discharged. Off-spec rejection = the share of discharged mass that was outside the moisture band, over the browning limit, or above Tmax (thermally degraded). Soft-sensor RMSE and ±2σ coverage compare the EKF moisture estimate with the true plant, skipping the first 5 minutes of start-up transient.

Pure client-side JavaScript, no libraries: physics, EKF, PID and MPC all run in this page. Simulated time advances in 5 s control/estimation steps; the MPC re-plans every 15 s.
